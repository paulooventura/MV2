AWDJOO TOWN 1.2 · TRIAL RUN

Water test: starts at 1.2 with all four jacks. Flooded room drops MIND in the lower corridor by the electro plug (shoot it with the TRS and the water rushes out and floods the corridor; D, the mirror enemy, patrols here). Water flows like Terraria: it falls, spreads and finds its level, and never vanishes — zap it with the TRS to boil it off as steam. Go to the water drops MIND on the ledge by the pool with the Isolator on (it doesn't wear off, but any hit knocks it off — grab it again; the crate regrows 30 s after you take it). Weather: boil water with the TRS — a little makes a haze; the bottom-left pool's worth brews a storm for 30 s (thunder building) and then it rains; boil the flooded room too and lightning strikes the high places (a crackle marks the spot a second before). Out in that storm MIND builds static and draws the bolts — get under cover. Boil a lot at once for a torrent. Under water, hold the XLR to blow an air pocket (3×3 blocks at full charge) and watch the pool rise and spill over the ledge; let go and it floats up and bursts. Hold the MAG under water to pull the surface down over its tip. Pick the look under the screen (A · SNES / B · Mega Drive / C · Posh). Click the game once to start the sound.
