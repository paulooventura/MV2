AWDJOO TOWN 1.3 · TRIAL RUN

Level 1.3 from your Oct 22 sheet, with all four jacks. The left shaft's green belts run forever (left DOWN, right UP); purple lifts go up and down. Enemy E (the eye) floats and fires a laser through its lens; Enemy F (the clear cube) sleeps in the pool until you swim near. The heptagon floats up when you come near the bowl and fires from its seven corners — only the gaucho knife's loaded slash hurts it (five fully heated 9-second slashes beat it). The knife waits in a secret room: drop down the shaft right of the arena and find the way in through its wall (item 5: tap X / Q to slash up and down, hold to heat it — it cuts the vines before the exit). It's a skyscraper site: the residential tower (left, its elevators run forever — left shafts down, right shafts up), the commercial tower (middle) and the high-tech tower (right). The high-tech tower hides a vault with the save-state item (S) and an Isolator — its only door is a secret one at its bottom right; secret rooms only show once you're inside. Pick the look under the screen. Click the game once to start the sound.
