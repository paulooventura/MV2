Water test: starts at 1.2 with all four jacks. Go to the water drops MIND on the ledge by the pool with the Isolator on (it lasts 30 s; the crate regrows 30 s after you take it). Under water, hold the XLR to blow an air pocket (3×3 blocks at full charge) and watch the pool rise and spill over the ledge; let go and it floats up and bursts. Hold the MAG under water to pull the surface down over its tip. Pick the look under the screen (A · SNES / B · Mega Drive / C · Posh). Click the game once to start the sound.
